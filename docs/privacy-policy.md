# YouTube Reimagined Privacy Policy

YouTube Reimagined runs in your browser. The bundled extension does not include analytics or send your settings, browsing history, or YouTube activity to the project maintainers.

## Data on your device

The extension uses browser-local storage for your preferences, enabled plugins, selected theme, module settings, and imported plugin and theme files, including theme previews. It may also store module errors to display them in the popup. On supported Chromium browsers, temporary data in browser session storage helps coordinate imported plugins.

Settings backups and mods archives are created on your device when you export them. A settings backup can contain your preferences and installed module files. Anyone you share that file with can read its contents.

## YouTube access and permissions

The extension has site access to `www.youtube.com` and `m.youtube.com`. It reads and modifies page content there to apply the plugins and theme you choose. The storage permission saves your configuration, and the scripting permission applies modifications to YouTube pages.

Compatible Chromium builds also use the User Scripts permission to run JavaScript plugins you import and enable. Third-party plugins can read or change YouTube page content and may send data to other services. Third-party themes may load external resources. Review third-party modules before installing or enabling them.

## External services

When you open the popup, the extension may request the latest public release from GitHub. It sends no credentials or extension settings and stores the result and any dismissal on your device.

Links to GitHub and Discord open those services in a browser tab. Those services handle your activity under their own privacy policies. If you post feedback or attach an export, the information you include is shared with that service and anyone who can view the post.

## Delete your data

Use **Settings → Reset → More reset options → Reset all data** to clear saved configuration and imported modules from the extension. You can also uninstall the extension to remove its browser-local data. Delete exported backups or mods archives separately.

## Questions

For questions about this policy, contact the project through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Antinity/youtube-reimagined/issues). Avoid including private information in public issues.
